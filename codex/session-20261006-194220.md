## Initial analysis

We reviewed `FinancialReportGenerator` and identified its intended role as the coordinator for monthly and yearly financial reports. Its workflow is intended to:

1. Retrieve revenue and expense transactions for a reporting period.
2. Calculate revenue, tips, expenses, estimated taxes, and take-home income.
3. Populate LaTeX report templates.
4. Invoke `pdflatex` to generate a PDF.
5. Email the generated PDF to configured recipients.

Reports can be requested through `SmsController` or generated by the monthly and yearly scheduled jobs.

## Decisions and skipped work

### PDF working-directory injection

We intentionally skipped correcting the `ProcessExecutionService` working-directory `@Value` expression because no desired directory/configuration behavior was provided for that item.

### Tip tax treatment

We established that tips are tax exempt. Tips therefore do not contribute to taxable gross income or estimated taxes, but they do contribute to take-home income.

The resulting calculation is:

```text
taxable income = revenue + expenses
estimated taxes = taxable income * 25%
take-home income = taxable income - estimated taxes + tips
```
